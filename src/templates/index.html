No document.
  • Open a PDF, or drop one onto the page.
  • Pick a shape and draw on the page. A polygon closes on double-click.
  • Drag a zone to move it, or its handles to resize it.
  • Right-click a zone to change its mode or delete it. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
  • From the keyboard: Tab moves between zones, Enter opens the zone menu, Delete removes it.
  • Ctrl+wheel or Ctrl with + / − / 0 zooms; both views zoom together. Drag with the middle button or hold Space to move around, arrows and Page Up/Down too.
  • What disappears follows the drawn outline, polygons and freehand included: nothing outside it is erased or covered.
  • Delete erases the content; Pixelate leaves nothing but an unreadable mosaic.
  • A delete zone is covered in the colour of the paper it was drawn on, averaged along its outline. Change it in the zone menu — three RGB numbers, or the pipette to take a colour from a click on the page.
  • Reloading the page keeps the document and its zones: they are picked up again as long as the tab stays open and the document has not expired on the server.
  • A watermark (typed in the field next to Export) is stamped diagonally across every exported page; a preview appears on the pages as you type.
